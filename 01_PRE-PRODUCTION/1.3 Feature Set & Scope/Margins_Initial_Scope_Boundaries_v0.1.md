# Margins Initial Scope Boundaries

## Purpose

Protect the first playable milestone from expanding into the complete long-term vision before the core progression loop is validated.

## Vertical-slice commitments

- One compact city block
- One fully operational business type
- At least two commercial locations with meaningfully different market conditions
- First-person receiving, stocking or preparation, checkout or service, cleaning, and basic maintenance
- Data-driven products or services
- Basic customer demand and satisfaction
- Employee hiring, scheduling, and task assignment
- At least two worker roles and one manager role
- Off-site business simulation
- Location-level and portfolio-level financial reporting
- Interior furniture and equipment placement on a grid
- Saving and loading the layout and business state

## Deferred

- Multiple complete business categories
- Player-built multi-story structures
- Runtime procedural generation of the full city
- Mixed-use development
- Detailed corporate departments
- Advanced franchising agreements
- Multiplayer
- Full economic cycles or stock-market simulation
- Deep competitor AI
- Every city building being enterable
- Fully persistent large crowds

## Technical constraint

Off-screen businesses should use aggregate simulation. Detailed NPCs and interactions should be instantiated only where the player is present or where a specific event requires them.

## Content constraint

The first business should be selected for system coverage and reuse. A convenience store or combined gas-station convenience store is a strong candidate because it exercises inventory, pricing, staffing, traffic, theft, maintenance, suppliers, and expansion.
