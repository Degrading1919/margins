# Margins Content and Commercial Strategy

## Status and authority

- **Status:** Current pre-production direction synchronized to the approved foundational decisions
- **Authority:** This document organizes approved content and commercial boundaries. It does not select unapproved businesses, districts, release dates, storefronts, prices, or marketing commitments.

## Content strategy

Margins should build a recognizable commercial world through reusable systems and modular content rather than a large quantity of unrelated bespoke assets.

The approved content foundation is:

- one fictional, contemporary American city;
- expanding handcrafted districts assembled from reusable modules;
- grounded light humor expressed through fictional brands, advertising, customers, employees, and local businesses;
- primarily systemic storytelling rather than a fixed protagonist campaign;
- persistent employees and managers;
- aggregate customer populations with a limited number of recurring customers;
- data-driven products, businesses, properties, customer contexts, events, and tuning; and
- physical and visual expression of financial growth through stores, properties, signage, headquarters, residences, vehicles, and amenities where later scope supports them.

## Vertical-slice content commitment

The vertical slice contains one complete standalone convenience-store category across at least two locations with different market conditions.

Content must be sufficient to prove:

- guided startup and store preparation;
- receiving, stocking, scanning or service, cleaning, and maintenance;
- products, fixtures, equipment, and inventory;
- at least two worker roles and one manager role;
- customers driven by local demand and satisfaction;
- understandable nearby competition;
- location differences;
- delegation, off-site simulation, and reporting;
- saved layouts and business state.

Exact product counts, brands, store themes, employee archetypes, customer archetypes, events, and district details remain unresolved.

## Business-category expansion

The 1.0 minimum targets at least two complete business categories, property ownership and development, and core holding-company progression.

The second business will be selected only after the convenience-store vertical slice is validated.

Approved shortlist:

- coffee shop;
- laundromat;
- card or hobby shop.

Selection must consider shared-system reuse, meaningful mechanical contrast, production cost, art and animation load, UI and engineering cost, delegation and off-site suitability, property and holding-company value, and player evidence.

No second business is currently selected, and three or more categories are not promised for 1.0.

## Content-production method

Content should be:

- modular and reusable;
- structured through approved data formats;
- validated before integration;
- authored through human, agentic, or hybrid workflows appropriate to the risk;
- reviewed in gameplay context rather than only as isolated assets or text;
- traceable to source, license, attribution, modification, and AI involvement where applicable.

The project uses a controlled hybrid asset pipeline. Self-created and properly licensed assets are preferred. Generative AI may support concepts, references, drafts, variations, automation, and bounded production. No generated asset class is automatically approved for shipping.

## Public modding boundary

Internal data-driven content and tools are approved from the beginning. Public mod support, public editors, compatibility guarantees, and Workshop integration are later commercial decisions and are not current commitments.

## Commercial path

The approved staged path is:

1. internal playable vertical slice;
2. polished public demo or controlled playtest;
3. evidence-driven stabilization;
4. possible paid Early Access only when the store-to-portfolio loop is enjoyable, stable, and replayable;
5. complete premium single-player release.

The game should not depend on pay-to-win systems or microtransactions.

Early Access is optional and quality-gated. The vertical slice does not automatically become a paid product.

## Explicitly unresolved

- city name, map, district roster, and full content plan;
- convenience-store catalog size and fictional brands;
- exact employee, customer, event, property, and supplier content;
- the second business category;
- 1.0 content quantity beyond the approved minimum;
- localization scope;
- voice, dialogue, and narrative-content depth;
- public mod support;
- release date, price, storefront, demo timing, or Early Access commitment;
- marketing channels, trailer plan, community strategy, or update cadence;
- post-1.0 content and sequel plans.

Content and commercial proposals remain subordinate to `Margins_Foundational_Decisions_v1.0.md` and require project-owner approval before becoming commitments.
