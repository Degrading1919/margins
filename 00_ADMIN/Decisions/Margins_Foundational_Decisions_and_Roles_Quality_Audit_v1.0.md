# Margins Foundational Decisions and Roles Quality Audit v1.0

## Status

- **Artifact type:** Validation and governance evidence
- **Audit target:** `Margins_Foundational_Decisions_v1.0.md`, `Margins_Assistant_Roles.md`, and `Margins_Assistant_Activation_Prompts.md`
- **Audit date:** July 26, 2026
- **Result:** Three gates passed after corrections
- **Authority note:** This report does not create new project decisions. It records checks against the project owner’s approved conversation and repository authority model.

## Audit standard

The audit required all target artifacts to satisfy the following:

1. preserve every explicit approval from the foundational decision session;
2. exclude rejected options, assistant-only speculation, and unapproved extrapolation;
3. preserve qualifiers such as vertical-slice, optional, phased, later, deferred, and unresolved;
4. distinguish long-term direction from milestone commitment;
5. maintain the project owner’s final authority;
6. keep roles as operating lenses rather than hidden carriers of project truth;
7. expose omissions, contradictions, stale lower-authority material, and future synchronization needs.

## Gate 2 — Role and activation consistency audit

### Method

The nine canonical role definitions were checked against FD-030, the role-and-skill model, the shared authority contract, and the complete foundational decision register. The activation prompts were then checked for exact role-name parity and proper routing to the canonical role document.

### Structural checks

All nine canonical roles are present with matching activation prompts:

1. Creative Director Assistant
2. Producer and Roadmap Assistant
3. Technical Architect Assistant
4. Systems and Simulation Designer Assistant
5. Economy and Progression Designer Assistant
6. Business and Content Designer Assistant
7. UX and Player-Experience Designer Assistant
8. Art and Presentation Director Assistant
9. Data, Validation, and QA Engineer Assistant

Every role contains:

- explicit activation;
- mission;
- required repository context;
- discipline lens;
- owned and recommended work;
- prohibited effects;
- project-owner authority;
- working method;
- expected outputs;
- handoffs.

### Semantic checks by role

- **Creative Director:** preserves the owner-operator-to-portfolio fantasy, cohesion, reuse, scope discipline, and project-owner authority.
- **Producer and Roadmap:** reflects solo development, AI-assisted workflows, budget and human-time constraints, durable vertical-slice work, and evidence-based completion.
- **Technical Architect:** preserves engine neutrality and prohibits silent engine, language, dependency, or architecture selection.
- **Systems and Simulation:** preserves detailed-versus-aggregate simulation, delegation, reporting, persistence, and shared-system reuse.
- **Economy and Progression:** preserves challenging-but-recoverable pressure, capability-based progression, recovery, private finance, competition, and long-term M&A direction.
- **Business and Content:** preserves distinct businesses on a shared foundation, tactile-to-delegated progression, grounded light humor, and business-category approval boundaries.
- **UX and Player Experience:** preserves tactile-but-assisted interaction, actionable reporting, remote-management boundaries, progressive disclosure, and hybrid-time needs.
- **Art and Presentation:** exactly preserves Stylized Contemporary Americana and the approved Road 96, Schedule I, and Firewatch reference responsibilities, plus the controlled provenance pipeline.
- **Data, Validation, and QA:** preserves data-driven authoring, schemas and validators, persistence safety, detailed/aggregate parity, evidence, and owner acceptance.

### Ambiguity checks

The following subjects appear in roles but are not falsely promoted into approved feature scope:

- **Accessibility and remapping:** role responsibility is to identify and evaluate requirements; exact feature scope remains unresolved and owner-approved.
- **Audio:** remains combined under Art and Presentation as approved by FD-030; detailed audio direction is unresolved.
- **M&A:** Economy and Progression treats it as long-term support; no milestone is assigned by the role.
- **Implementation:** no role is granted autonomous implementation or publication authority; implementation remains separately authorized.
- **Licensing and provenance:** roles may review and report, but only the project owner can accept risk or approve shipped assets.

### Activation-prompt checks

- All nine activation prompts use the same canonical role names.
- Every prompt requires reading `Margins_Assistant_Roles.md` and current repository authority.
- No prompt duplicates enough role content to become a competing source of truth.
- Every prompt preserves project-owner authority and role-specific prohibitions.

### Gate 2 disposition

**PASS.** The roles and activation prompts are structurally complete, semantically consistent with the approved decisions, and do not claim unapproved authority or milestone commitments. No role-file correction was required after the full comparison.

## Gate 3 — Adversarial completeness and contradiction audit

### Method

The foundation was reviewed as if by a hostile downstream assistant attempting to infer extra scope, select unresolved technology, or turn aspirations into roadmap commitments. The audit searched for omissions, contradictory milestones, stale source conflicts, hidden feature commitments, and authority leakage.

### Issues found and corrected

1. **Vertical-slice staffing completeness:** The final decision record explicitly preserves the existing requirement for at least two worker roles and one manager role.
2. **Property milestone reconciliation:** FD-009 now explicitly acknowledges FD-021’s separate 1.0 property commitment while leaving exact construction depth unresolved.
3. **Early Access leakage:** Early Access is explicitly optional and quality-gated, not guaranteed.
4. **Driving leakage:** Drivable vehicles are explicitly prototype-gated, not a vertical-slice or unconditional later commitment.
5. **M&A leakage:** M&A is an approved long-term direction without a committed milestone or transaction-depth model.
6. **Second-business leakage:** The shortlist is approved; no category is selected.
7. **Technology leakage:** Engine, language, rendering stack, and architecture remain unselected.
8. **Modding leakage:** Data-driven internal architecture is approved; public mod support and compatibility are not.
9. **Art-reference leakage:** Reference games define selected qualities only and do not authorize imitation.
10. **Role-authority leakage:** Roles cannot approve their own recommendations, rewrite project truth, publish changes, or declare acceptance.

### Completeness checks

The decision record now contains explicit unresolved boundaries for:

- technology and performance;
- second business and city content;
- tuning and progression thresholds;
- competition, finance, M&A, and property depth;
- vehicles;
- mod support;
- public markets;
- art, audio, animation, and asset budgets;
- accessibility scope;
- release timing, pricing, storefronts, and marketing;
- roadmap stages and schedules;
- post-1.0 promises.

### Remaining cross-repository risk

The approved decisions and roles pass, but lower-authority pre-production documents are stale in places:

- the project brief still says the direction is not locked;
- the initial scope document still describes the first business as a convenience-store or gas-station candidate;
- several planned pre-production domains do not yet contain the approved art, economy, content, technical, and commercial decisions.

These are not contradictions inside the approved decision register or roles because the authority hierarchy resolves them. They are a **required synchronization task before roadmap authoring treats the repository as fully aligned**.

### Gate 3 disposition

**PASS WITH REQUIRED NEXT ACTION.** The audited decisions and roles are accurate, complete for the approved foundational session, and protected against the identified hallucination and scope-creep paths. The first roadmap must not be treated as baseline-ready until the stale lower-authority pre-production documents are synchronized to the approved decision record.
